Welcome

Come hungry, leave family.

Dragon House is a family restaurant on Cuauhtemoc in the Centro of Puerto Penasco. We cook the Chinese and Cantonese dishes our family loves, the kind made to share. Our plates come piled high, our dining room glows with red lanterns and gold, and there is always a seat for you. Dine in with us, or call and we will bring it to your door.

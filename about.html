Our House

A family kitchen in the heart of Puerto Penasco.

Nosotros

Real food, made well.

Dragon House is a family kitchen. We cook Chinese and Cantonese dishes the way they should be cooked, by hand and with care, the recipes our family has always made. Chun Kung rolls, carnitas coloradas, chow mein, fresh shrimp, soups that warm you up. Nothing fancy, just real food made well.

Step inside and you will see why people stay. Red paper lanterns glow over the tables. A gold fortune mural runs along the wall. A jade laughing Buddha and a gold lucky cat keep watch by the door, and the dragon watches over all of it. It is warm, it is bright, and it feels like home.

We believe a good meal should be generous. Our plates come full, our combos are built to share, and there is always room for one more at the table. Whether you are a neighbor from the Centro or visiting Puerto Penasco for the first time, you are welcome here.
